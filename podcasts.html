An Extension of Women of MP3.com

This podcast archive grew directly out of the work at Women of MP3.com. Where the channel's CyberMuse column focused on women artists on the MP3.com platform, the podcast conversations extended the same editorial curiosity into audio — and to performers of all genders.

The same values drove both: independent music, direct conversation, genuine interest in how artists make and sustain their work. These 56 episodes were hosted on the Internet Archive and span the same era as the channel — from indie singer-songwriters to jazz musicians to alt-rock bands to electronic producers.
