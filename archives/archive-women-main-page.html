Celtic Women Unite!
The Gathering of Celtic Women speaks for itself. Celtic women joined together to promote peace, education and culture in Ireland, and to promote networking of Celts in general and strengthen once again the tribe that has been scattered across the planet.

The Gathering I was conceived back in fall '99, and slowly came together over the next few months. By the beginning of the new millennium it was up and running at MP3.com. The original goal was to network among Celtic musicians, both traditional and non-traditional, to help generate traffic and promote the music from "the soul of the Celtic Nation." When the CD started to sell, another goal became obvious: to give all of the money, the artists' portion of the CD sales and the Payback for Playback money to The Ireland Funds Organization to promote peace, reconciliation, culture, art, music and education in Ireland. Since the inception of The Gathering, the group has raised more than $2,400 for Ireland Funds. Check it out!

Lorraine Lawson
"Do"
Lo Fi: Play


From age 2 at the neighborhood Christmas pageant, Lorraine Lawson has been singing and dancing morning, noon and night. Since then, Lorraine has performed in front of thousands at venues from the Calgary Olympic Games to the YTV Achievement Awards broadcast across Canada. Most recently, Lorraine has been featured as the national anthem singer for the NBA's Toronto Raptors and the NHL's Toronto Maple Leafs. While gigging in the Toronto area, she independently released the current CD of new tunes to Toronto retailers and met with phenomenal success. Currently getting airplay on three top CHR radio stations in Toronto--Kiss 92, Energy 108 and Hits 103.5--the CD has been pushed to the top of the HMV retail charts for indie and major-label CDs.

About "Do":
"This song is a very sexy little number that encourages you to be yourself and 'do what you wanna do...' Written during a well-deserved break in Florida (on holidays at the beach, in the pool and later, up in the hotel room), the song wrote itself as Lorraine and Karon tossed verses back and forth while chillin' out by the pool."

Cyber Muse by Jianda Johnson


Denise Tichenor-Collins
"REVELATIONS"
Lo Fi: Play
With a modern verve, Denise Tichenor-Collins takes an old-time Gospel flava and mixes in smooth, well-produced melodies and R&B arrangements. A walk through her release leads us first to "Jazzlude," a slick voice-as-instrument jam that's nearly 100% classic scat. "Near the Cross," aside from its testimony of pure faith, is as slick and soulful as any TLC cut, while the Gospel-Funk song "There's no Me" would fit right in alongside any Winans family album. Tichenor-Collins displays both her talent, and her loving spirit in this release, which, like Bebe and Cece Winans' tunes, would fare just as well on Top 40 stations as it would on Christian radio. If Soul or Gospel strikes your fancy, this indie EP is a keeper!

Video Diary - Qes k'say




"Adam Was A Hologram"
Download
Real Video (1,871kb)
(2:01 minutes)



You'll need Real Player plugin to view this video.


This video clip was produced for the Qes k'say Enhanced CD "Girl Talk." It was self-produced by Joan Ludmer (a.k.a. Joanzie) along with her hubby Mark and shot entirely in their Goleta, California, condominium using a DV camcorder. No special lighting was used, just good old on-location rawness. The editing and special effects were done on Joanzie's home PC, the same computer that recorded and mixed the music, and created the multimedia portions for the enhanced CD.

The song was written by Joan and is about a superficial friend "Adam," who possessed a great amount of charm and wit, but lacked any real substance. Adam's character is simulated in the video clip with a 3-D animated cartoon entity and is in no way related to the Biblical partner of Eve.
