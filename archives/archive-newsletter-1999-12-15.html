Holly Figueroa, Prevention Instead of Recovery back to top...

Indiegrrl is releasing a benefit compilation CD in mid- March of 2000. What's so special about that? The benefactors will be the Men's Rape Prevention Project. The project focuses on rape as a men's issue and a women's issue. It educates men and young boys across the country about rape prevention, warning signs within themselves and their community, and other issues.

"MRPP focuses on prevention instead of recovery. What America is focused on doing now is supporting women after the fact, which is wonderful. As a survivor myself, I applaud organizations like RAINN and local support groups. But rape is not a women's issue, it is a human issue. Teaching women how to defend themselves against rapists is crucial, but we should also teach men how not to rape." -Holly Figueroa
All proceeds of the upcoming compilation go toward MRPP. In addition, members of Indiegrrl are encouraged to volunteer for the organization as well if there is a branch in their area...and if not, they can create one. Men Against Rape is a new concept, but many grassroots organizations are popping up all over the country. Show your support by helping too! The submission deadline for this compilation has passed, but Indiegrrl plans to release at least two comps per year, so there always are upcoming opportunities. To get involved, please visit Indiegrrl.

Virginia Norwood - Gigs for Good back to top...

Name/Band/Position: Virginia Norwood from Beyond Blue, Lead Vocalist, Keyboard Player

Musical Background: 19 years of performing in bands doing covers and originals; B.A. in Music from San Diego State University; teacher of vocal technique; several years of private study in vocal technique; some piano and guitar.

When did you become interested in music: "I've been interested in music since childhood. I was always singing by myself and with my sister and cousin. My father bought me an acoustic guitar when I was in third grade. I had an eight week course which taught me some basic chords and songs like, 'Country Roads' and 'Leavin' on a Jet Plane'. We used to have a blast with that guitar and just sing at the top of our lungs! My mom probably wished I had a kazoo instead. I was too shy to perform in public, so being a performer was only a dream until my late teen-age years. I think it must have been hearing 'Sweet Home Alabama' by Lynyrd Skynyrd that fueled the fire in me with the three ladies on backing vocals. Then I heard Ann Wilson (Heart) and my dream became an obsession. I took some voice classes at the junior college, and joined my first band (a heavy-metal band!).

How are you using your music to help others in need? Beyond Blue performs for a lot of charity functions. We recently performed at the Rock 4-Xmas charity event which raised over $2,000 for needy families. On the 11th we performed at a fund-raiser for the Girls Club of San Diego. Last year we performed at the Whitney Rae Pappas Benefit Concert and Tune-Up for Youth Benefit. I use my student recitals as fund-raisers for charity. The last one raised almost $400 for the Girls Club of San Diego. My sister is a teacher now and from time to time I'll pop into her classroom and sing for the first-graders. You should see their little faces light up!

Weep: All About Giving back to top...

"About a year and a half a go, WEEP began a crusade. All of the proceeds from the sale of their CD 'Soul Crying', is donated to help aid the fight against breast cancer. In addition, WEEP matches the amount they make in their sales at live performances, as well as giving out information provided by the National Breast Cancer Coalition. So far we have raised a good amount, and continue to do so to this day.

Soon we will be releasing our second CD, 'Moment in Time', and will donate a percentage of our sales to another cause, yet to be decided. On Christmas of this year WEEP will be donating their time as a group to their local 'Loaves and Fishes' organization to feed the hungry and make sure they get a good Christmas meal. They will also be taking up of collection of either toys, or money to buy toys, for the local 'Toys for Tots' program.

WEEP is all about giving. The biggest thing we can give is our music, which is written to inspire and to help people, and to let them know that there is always hope." -Kim Paschke

To find out how you can help, please visit: National Breast Cancer Coalition * Loaves and Fishes of Tompkins County * Toys for Tots

Evangel and M.O.R. Rally for Peace back to top...

Imagine being in a world where you were asked to make a decision. A decision between believing in a religion you've always known, or being forced to convert to a religion you've never accepted as true. If you do not accept this religion, you will be killed and your children will be sent into slavery if you do not denounce your faith. You choose not to accept and you are drowned, shot or beheaded.

On Nov. 12, 1999, Evangel & M.O.R. were contacted by an organization that has launched a refugee recovery campaign. The Sudan Recovery and Relief now has in its possession more than 45 children whose parents were killed in Sudan. Evangel has been asked to write a national theme song for Sudan Recovery and Relief, all proceeds going toward the campaign. In addition, Evangel and M.O.R. have launched a campaign for the city of San Diego to help support and rally for peace. They have been hosting charitable concerts and raising money for needy families back in Africa.

Do you want to help? Learn more at Urgent Appeal for Financial Assistance for the Sudan.

Pop Songstress Tyler O'Neil Sings for the Pope! back to top...

Tyler O'Neil usually performs in small clubs, outdoor festivals and restaurants with bars. But this Christmas, she's in for the gig of her life as she'll be flown to Rome to sing for Pope John Paul II.

It's going to be a wonderful experience!" says O'Neil. "I can't wait to see the Pope and see where St. Peter was buried. Amazing!"

O'Neil will be singing with a choir of 200 people, rehearsing Dec. 23 and 24, then performing at the Vatican on Christmas Day. The performance will be televised worldwide.

Naturally, we had to ask how all of this came about: "I got the gig because I did a show at Highland Grounds in L.A. last June. One of the guys in the audience really enjoyed our performance. His name is Darryl Phinnesse and he sings background vocals for people like Michael Jackson, Lionel Richie and Anita Baker and we've kept in touch since the show. He called me about a month ago and said the people coordinating the event wanted me on board. I'm so excited about it!"

And MP3.com is just as excited. O'Neil's D.A.M. CD, "Ten Out of Tyler," is a mellow pop melding of exquisite vocals and sparkling melodies. No surprise the songs delighted the Pope's 'people'.
