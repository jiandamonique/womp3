Alexander Scott's Success Story

I don't know how to measure 'success' per se, but I have found that the benefits of having my music here on the site are huge and seem to be (knock wood) growing. I've gotten five dates on the Music and Technology Tour, which is huge for me, and interest from some pretty big-time lawyers and managers. I have also gotten so many emails from people who liked the songs (fortunately I haven't yet gotten any saying how awful someone thinks I am). It's really thrilling to hear from someone in Australia or England or France, and it gives me a little bit of faith in the much-touted global community of the Net. I'm sure I'm not the only person who started writing songs out of pervasive teenage isolation and loneliness, so I hope that whoever reads this will understand exactly why the opportunity to be heard by and to hear from so many people is truly amazing.
