WOMP3 Guide to
Hip Hop/Rap 		  
Having a hard time finding quality female hip hop artists? BgyrL4Life, aka SoFly I-N-I from the popular b-gYrl web sites, has put together a guide giving you a taste of the best hip hop/rap on MP3.com and the web.
Female Pioneers

Bella Mofia Coming with the hardcore and more! Three of New York's hottest female rapper,s RASHERA, JIG LACEY and CHEN'NA linked up to form Bella Mafia, a trio known for tearing up the underground scene in New York City. Thug Passion at its best! Could the Source, Jet, UpScale, Vibe, Black Beat, Essence, Notorious, Blaze, XXX, and Ebony magazines all be wrong?


Rha Goddess A hip hop artist for the next millennium. Rha Goddess has her roots in traditional rap, but also incorporates jazz and rock elements in her music. She is a true lyricist, who can also make your head nod. Rha Goddess, the former international spokesperson for the Zulu Nation, has been active in New York City's underground rap and spoken-word community for several years. She has shared the stage with the likes of KRS-One, Chuck D, The Last Poets and poet Saul Williams (the movie.)


Princess Don't trip off the skin tone. Princess delivers "raw heat," with deep and well-written lyrics, complemented by a versatile flow style. She credits her success to unlimited hours of studio time that allowed her to experiment, thus enhancing her creativity. Over the past year, Princess has been getting her feet "soaking wet" learning the business end of the music industry and with all said and done, the only thing she's 100 percent sure of is that she was "put on this planet to rap."


Shining Star Looking for a female battle mc? Look no further. Shining Star is straight-settin' it: rappin' about her lyrical skills, other female rap artists (Foxy Brown, Eve and Little Kim), and challenging any male mc foolish enough to test her on the mic. And to any male thinking of wishing on a star: You better come with the riches because she won't settle for anything less.

Vika
Blowin' up the spot in A-Town! Vika reminds us that no matter how good your intentions, it's all about the benjamins and doin' "whatcha gotta do to get what ya gotta get." This is for all the heads who "keep it real" on a regular.



The Real Roxanne What more can anyone say that hasn't already been said about The Real Roxanne?

Outside Pages

queendom
The beginning of a new era in hip hop. The first show of its kind in hip hop her-story! Focusing on women in various facets of hip hop. Queendom is a forum to educate, uplift and entertain with a female slant. The show features females making and taking their roles in hip hop culture while discussing everything from politics to beats and sexism.
