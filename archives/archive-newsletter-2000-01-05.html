Cyber Muse Weekly back to top...

Pamela Zero
Given Time
by Jianda Johnson
That's it-- I've been bewitched. As Pamela Zero sings, "Ghosts have somehow moved into my days." I downloaded her MP3s, two at a time. "Entrance" beckoned (think: toning in caves) and "The Yearning" begged me to stay (see: Kate Bush, "Breathing"). Audio surfaced, first in 1 track, then 2, and I let them play simultaneously. "Physics" came in-the last track in this Jungian round--and it felt like cloud-busting. In Zero's tunes you'll find snippets of Miranda Sex Garden and Dead Can Dance. Did I mention her work is 99.9% a cappella? The arrangements and vocals are so lush, you forget-her larynx IS the instrument. Listening to Zero is like eavesdropping on someone's psyche. Catharsis follows, pure as church bells. Lest you think there's no music in the lot, the blues tune "The Strip Song" carries with it a nice, lively piano accompaniment. Otherwise, it's unfettered coloratura and bass vox, Baby. Enjoy.

Jianda Johnson is embarrassed to admit her first attempts to access Zero's tracks were straight up "Download Bleeps and Blunders." (Can you say "unable to open file") Bemused? Bothered? Bewildered? Check out W0'MP3's digital delights and send your feedback to Jianda c/o womenartists@mp3.com.

gilli moon is an expressionist back to top...

...an energy, charisma and passion that leave an audience wanting more, as the front person for the hot new band, Jessica Christ, currently taking Los Angeles by storm.

Born in Milan, Italy, growing up in the Australian bush, she began classical piano at the tender age of 5 and by 15 she had become an accomplished songwriter. Her songs have now gained acclaim with audiences worldwide- gracing television, film and theater production. gilli's songs have also found favor with other recording artists and have been featured on several major recordings.

As a newcomer to Los Angeles, gilli was acknowledged in her first year,as one of the "Top 100 Unsigned Artists of Southern California" by L.A's Music Connection magazine. gilli has also won a string of worldwide songwriting awards and was featured on MTV's "The Cut" as newly discovered talent.

We asked gilli to tell us a little about herself and what makes her tick:

Name/instrument? gilli moon singer/songwriter/pianist/music producer

How did you get started in the music biz? began as a child playing Dorothy in the "Wizard of Oz"

What inspires you? traveling, relationships (the good and the ugly), life's plans...especially when we aren't looking!

What have you done that you wish you had done differently? put out albums much earlier in life!

What advice would you give others? be autonomous. be a self-starter. believe not only in your talent, but your visions and follow them, even when you have to get out there alone and do it!

What is the high point of your career? being asked to be featured on the Women of MP3.com page!

Do you think men and women experience the music biz differently? no. I think youth vs. experience is the difference.

What is in the future for you? recording my music and making my life more and more a total creative experience.
